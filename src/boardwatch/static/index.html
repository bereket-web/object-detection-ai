Data & limits

59 rights-attributed images; 30 train, 9 validation, 20 group-held-out test.

Keep expectations narrow. The dataset is small and top-down only. Angled views, other board styles, lighting, and real-world objects need additional data and evaluation.

The demo runs locally. It does not send images to an external inference service.
